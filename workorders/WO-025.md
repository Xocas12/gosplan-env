## Implementation notes

Provisional until the lead revises the spec.

Rule, verbatim (PLAN §2.14):

```
R̃_i = π · R_i + (1 − π) · [ R̄_i^{prev} + κ_m · max(0, T_i − R_i) ]
```

with `π = information.ministry_passthrough` (1 = transparent), `R_i = view.claims`,
`T_i = view.targets`, `R̄_i^{prev} = view.prev_forward`. Two effects, both named by PLAN: the
`(1 − π) · R̄^{prev}` term **smooths** toward what this ministry forwarded last period — which is
what makes the layer an information mechanism, and what the republic-level anchor of PLAN §7.3
needs; the `κ_m · max(0, T_i − R_i)` term **pads a reported shortfall**, and only a shortfall.

What PLAN does **not** decide, and what the revision must state before this card is issued:

- `κ_m` — its existence as a configuration field, its arm (INFO by construction), its Phase-2 value
  and its sweep range. PLAN §3 has no row for it. No caller may assume one;
- the partition rule for `n_ministries > 1` (by sector, contiguous by index, or configured) and
  whether a ministry may span sectors;
- the Phase-2 value of `ministry_passthrough` in the C0 configuration (PLAN §4.3 fixes only the
  C_OGAS move, `→ 1`);
- the initial value of `prev_forward` at `t = 0`;
- what the audit sees when a ministry has altered the claim: PLAN §2.8 audits the claim against
  stock on hand, and whose claim is audited when the two differ is not written down.

Manifest (CONTRACT rule 10): a run with an active ministry records it, so a ministry-level series
can never be mistaken for an enterprise-level one in PLAN §7.3.
