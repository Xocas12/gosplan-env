Implementation notes

  1. GRANULARITY. One `StepRecord` per ENTERPRISE per AGENT-STEP - `M + 1` steps per period, `N`
     rows each. `StepInfo.records` arrives from the environment in enterprise-index order; the
     ledger APPENDS, it never recomputes.

  2. COLUMNS. The `State` fields of PLAN section 2.2 plus, verbatim from the WO-011 card of PLAN
     section 12.3: `y, R, rho, S_pre, S_post, audited, S_hat, f, Pen, fill, deliv, consumer,
     val_measured, val_true, welfare, at_bound`. `S_pre` is the stock BEFORE the holding loss and
     this period's output are applied, `S_post` after; the pair is what makes the T-U1 conservation
     identity checkable from the ledger alone. `at_bound` is the per-report boolean
     `report_ratio >= cfg.tech.report_max_ratio`.

  3. `bound_binding` (rule 8). True when the fraction of REPORT-step rows with `at_bound` exceeds
     `AT_BOUND_FLAG_THRESHOLD` (0.01). The flag name is `BOUND_BINDING_FLAG`, spelled once in the
     module so no caller invents a variant. It rides in the manifest's `flags` field and every
     report of that run displays it. BOUNDS ARE RESULTS: the threshold, the bound and the at-bound
     rows are never adjusted, trimmed or winsorised to clear a flag.

  4. `write_manifest(run_dir, cfg, extra)`. Writes `runs/<cfg.hash()>/manifest.json` with EXACTLY
     the keys of `MANIFEST_FIELDS`, in that order: `config_hash`, `config`, `spec_version`,
     `git_hash`, `seed_env`, `seed_policy`, `reference_ppo_version`, `estimator_version`,
     `estimator_backend`, `llm_models`, `solver`, `solver_version`, `solver_optimality_gap`,
     `bunching_settings`, `flags`. A field that does not apply to the run is written as `null`,
     NEVER OMITTED - a missing key is always a bug and never an ambiguity. An unknown key in `extra`
     is an error, not a silent addition. `git_hash` carries a dirty marker when the working tree is
     not clean.

  5. SERIALISATION. Parquet via `pyarrow`, imported INSIDE the function that uses it, never at
     module scope; `pandas` likewise for `to_dataframe`. `float("inf")` inside the embedded `config`
     serialises as the string `"inf"`, matching `EnvConfig.hash`'s canonical JSON encoding (WO-003),
     so the two encoders agree. `runs/**/*.parquet` is git-ignored; `manifest.json` is not.

  6. DIRECTION OF INFORMATION (rule 6). The ledger sits strictly downstream of the environment:
     `gosplan/metrics/` imports nothing from `gosplan/env/`, which is why the `Phase` literal is
     restated in this module rather than imported. Nothing here is ever read by an agent, an
     observation or a reward term. Its inputs are exactly the true quantities the environment logs -
     `welfare`, `val_true` and `val_measured` among them.

  7. MEASUREMENT WINDOW. The ledger writes EVERY row, including `t_period < 2`. The burn-in of PLAN
     section 4.4 is applied by the READER (`gosplan/metrics/phenomena.py`, WO-016), never by the
     writer; dropping rows here would make the conservation identity uncheckable.

Forbidden

  - Computing, plotting or asserting any quantity from the PLAN section 4.1 phenomena table - ROWS
    2, 5, 6 AND 7 ABOVE ALL (storming, hoarding, blat, hidden reserves), which are held out until
    the Phase-2 acceptance run. The ledger records the raw columns those rows will one day be
    computed from; it computes no phenomenon at all. Rows 1 and 4 belong to WO-016.
  - Dropping, trimming, winsorising or de-duplicating at-bound rows; widening
    `AT_BOUND_FLAG_THRESHOLD` or `report_max_ratio` (rule 8).
  - Omitting an inapplicable manifest field instead of writing `null`; adding a key that is not in
    `MANIFEST_FIELDS` (changing that tuple needs an AMBIGUITY REPORT and a lead decision).
  - Importing anything from `gosplan/env/` into `gosplan/metrics/`; importing `pyarrow`, `pandas` or
    `forensics_core` at module scope.
  - Letting any agent, policy or reward term read the `Ledger` or `StepInfo` (rule 6).
  - Applying the PLAN section 4.4 window, or any filter, at write time.
  - Touching `tests/`, `ref/`, `spec/spec.py`, `gosplan/metrics/__init__.py` or `PLAN.md`. Running
    `tests/acceptance/` (rule 13).
