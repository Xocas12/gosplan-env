Implementation notes

  `spec/spec.py`. A full expansion of PLAN section 10, not a copy of it: typed fields, complete
  docstrings, and EVERY function body exactly

      raise NotImplementedError("<PLAN section> - implemented in WO-###")

  with no logic of any kind. What counts as real content here, and is required rather than
  forbidden: the type aliases and literal enums; the four configuration dataclasses with their
  Phase-1 FIELD DEFAULTS from PLAN section 3; the record types `State`, `EnterpriseAction`,
  `PlannerView`, `MinistryView`, `StepInfo`, `StepRecord`, `DPGrid`, `DPSolution`; the `Agent`
  Protocol; and the docstring of every public symbol. Dataclasses get fields only: no
  `__post_init__` body, no computed property.

  Conventions the file must establish, because every later module inherits them:
    - `Array = np.ndarray`, so the Phase-2 JAX port substitutes its own array type behind one name;
      no signature may depend on a numpy-only method.
    - Struct-of-arrays with leading dimension `N` throughout (PLAN section 10 preamble), so WO-029
      is a mechanical translation.
    - `SPEC_VERSION = "0.1.0"`. WO-013 bumps it to "1.0.0" at the v1 freeze; after that, CONTRACT
      rule 1 requires a `spec/CHANGELOG.md` entry for every change.
    - Configuration dataclasses are `frozen=True` and hashable, so every array-valued default is a
      `tuple` and never a list or an ndarray. `EnvConfig` must be usable as a dictionary key and
      must hash reproducibly, because `EnvConfig.hash()` names the run directory (CONTRACT rule 10).
    - Every docstring states what the symbol takes, what it returns, the exact PLAN formula or
      section it must follow, and the test ids (T-U#/T-B#) or acceptance criteria that bind it. A
      mid-tier implementer must not need PLAN.md to know what to write.

  Where PLAN section 10 types something loosely, narrowing is allowed but must be recorded. The two
  known narrowings: `draw`'s `purpose` and `dist` arguments are typed `str` in PLAN section 10 and
  are narrowed to the `Purpose` and `Dist` literals, which enumerate exactly the values PLAN section
  2.15 and the WO-004 card name. Note the narrowing in the docstring and record it in
  `spec/CHANGELOG.md` at the v1 freeze (WO-013), not here.

  `CONTRACT.md` is PLAN section 9 VERBATIM: thirteen numbered rules, no additions, no softening, no
  renumbering. Later cards quote it by number, so a renumbering silently invalidates every card.

  `gosplan/params.py` is the PLAN section 3 table as DATA, not as prose: one record per parameter
  with `arm` (info / inc / supply / tech), `phase`, `default`, `range` and `source`. Notes:
    - The arm classification is a design decision (CONTRACT rule 11). `audit_rate` is dual - it is
      an INFO parameter that also enters the reward through the penalty - and must be recorded as
      such, because PLAN section 4.3 reports it separately and never folds it into C_OGAS.
    - The six provisional rows - `ratchet_lambda`, `growth_directive`, `overfulfilment_slope`,
      `penalty_scale`, `effort_cost`, `audit_rate` - carry their placeholder default AND a flag
      saying the value is replaced at G1 from the DP regime map (PLAN sections 5, 13).
    - A range is either an interval or a discrete choice set; both forms need a type, since WO-003
      validates against them and WO-033 samples from them.
    - `source` comes from `docs/params_sources.md`, verbatim in substance: a sourced range, or the
      unsourced prior sentence. Never invent a source here.
    - The registry and the dataclass defaults in `spec/spec.py` are two encodings of one table and
      must agree field by field; `tests/unit/test_config.py` (WO-003) asserts it against
      `p1_default_config()`, so a drift introduced here surfaces two cards later.

  `workorders/TEMPLATE.md` and `workorders/AMBIGUITY_TEMPLATE.md` are the two code-fenced templates
  of PLAN section 12.1, verbatim, as the file body and not wrapped in a fence. Every later card
  fills the TEMPLATE fields completely.

Forbidden

  - Implementing any function body. Every body is exactly the one-line `raise NotImplementedError`.
    No helper arithmetic, no partial implementation, no magic number inside a body.
  - Adding a parameter that is not in PLAN section 3, removing one that is, or changing any
    parameter's arm assignment (CONTRACT rule 11 requires a CHANGELOG entry, and this card is
    before v1 so the registry is being created, not amended - if PLAN section 3 and PLAN section 10
    disagree about a value, that is an AMBIGUITY REPORT, not a judgement call).
  - Writing, editing or generating any test, any `ref/` file, or any module under `gosplan/` other
    than `params.py`.
  - Importing scipy, pandas, jax or torch at module scope in `spec/spec.py`. Reference them in
    docstrings only. Module-scope imports are limited to numpy, dataclasses, typing, enum, pathlib
    and intra-repo modules.
  - Importing `forensics_core` anywhere except inside a `try/except ImportError` that falls back to
    `gosplan.metrics._fallback`, exactly as PLAN section 7.3 specifies. `spec/spec.py` does not
    import it at all.
  - Writing anything, in code or in a docstring or in an example, that violates CONTRACT rules 4
    (reward terms), 5 (planner blindness), 6 (welfare blindness), 7 (no hard-coded pathology) or 9
    (RNG). A docstring that shows a shaping term as an example is a rule-4 violation.
  - Editing `PLAN.md`, or un-ignoring it.
