## Implementation notes

Provisional until the lead revises the spec.

**Row 2 — storming (held out, emergence).** `Gini_k(e_ik)` of *effort* across the steps within a
period, **minus** the same Gini for the truthful-myopic heuristic under **the same delivery-timing
draws** (common random numbers, shared `seed_env`). Positive excess = agents defer effort beyond
what input arrival forces. The comparison ledger is a required argument, not a convenience: the
baseline absorbs the mechanical concentration stochastic arrival alone produces. Estimator: own.
Locked parameters (PLAN §4.2): `delivery_timing = "stochastic"`,
`arrival_probs = (0.25, 0.25, 0.25, 0.25)` over steps 0..3 — uniform-random arrival, so there is no
mechanical backloading and any Gini excess is behavioural; `yield_sigma` ×1.

**Row 3 — quality degradation (pipeline check, *not* held out, *not* an emergence claim).** Mean
`q̄` under `objective_metric = "val"` against the same configuration at
`objective_metric = "quality_weighted"` with `quality_measurability` `μ = 1`. Estimator: own. The
mechanism is WO-021's.

**Row 5 — hoarding → shortage (held out, emergence).** Request inflation `q_ij / need_ij`, and
`corr(X_ij, 1 − fill_downstream)`; both against the truthful-myopic baseline under the same draws.
Estimator: `forensics_core.dispersion` for the cross-sectional part, resolved through
`gosplan.metrics.resolve_estimators()` (PLAN §7.3), with sectors as groups.
Locked parameters (PLAN §4.2): `alloc_eta_request` `η_q = 0.7`, `input_complementarity` `θ = 8`,
`input_holding_loss` `h_X = 0.01`.

**Row 6 — blat (held out, emergence).** Executed trade volume divided by total intermediate
allocation, over the measurement window; it must exceed the truthful-myopic baseline, which never
trades — so a non-zero share is the whole claim. Estimator: own.
Locked parameters (PLAN §4.2): `horizontal_visibility = 1.0`, `trade_tau` `τ = 0.05`.

**Row 7 — hidden reserves / shaving (held out, emergence).** `mean_i max(0, S_i − R_i) / T_i` after
delivery; it must vanish when `g = 0` and `penalty_arg = absolute` — that vanishing is part of the
pre-registered claim and is itself reported. Estimator: own, plus `forensics_core.reconciliation` on
the ledger through `resolve_estimators()`.
Locked parameters (PLAN §4.2): as Phase 1 — `growth_directive` `g = 0.02` (a daggered value, so
whatever `runs/G1_decision.md` recorded), `penalty_arg = "positive_part"`, `holding_loss` `h =
0.02`.

**Measurement window for all five (PLAN §4.4):** periods `t ≥ 2`; no end-of-episode exclusion under
geometric termination; reports at `ρ_max` are **included** in histograms and flagged.

**Agents (PLAN §6.1)** — baselines and comparators, never evidence of emergence:

```
Berliner   safety-factor rule: aims 5–10% above target, reports at target, banks the rest
Weitzman   ratchet-aware effort reduction as a function of λ
Kornai     request inflation factor, bailout-anticipating
```

These are **agents**, in `gosplan/agents/heuristic.py`, and nothing about them may migrate into
`gosplan/env/`: CONTRACT rule 7 forbids a transition rule or reward term that implements a
pathology, and a heuristic agent that plays a named historical pattern is a comparator for the
learners, not a demonstration that the environment produces the pattern. PLAN §6.1 fixes their
descriptions; the numeric details (the 5–10% band, the effort-reduction function, the inflation
factor) are not written down and the revision must supply them.

What PLAN does **not** decide, and what the revision must state before this card is issued:

- the Gini estimator's exact definition and its treatment of a period with zero total effort;
- which ledger columns carry `need`, `trade_volume`, `alloc` and `fill_downstream`, and at what
  granularity (WO-011 lists the Phase-1 columns; the Phase-2 additions come with WO-021…WO-025);
- the correlation's index set in row 5 (over `(i, j)` pairs, over enterprises, or per good);
- the numeric constants of the three heuristic agents named above.
