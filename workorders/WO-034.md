## Implementation notes

Provisional until the lead revises the spec.

**Why the DP is the anchor.** PLAN §5 lists "the exact no-manipulation counterfactual for the
estimator-bias study (§7.2)" among the DP's uses. The DP gives a distribution whose excess mass is
known analytically, so a bias curve measured against it is a bias and not a disagreement between two
estimators.

**Why `w` is the x-axis.** PLAN §2.8 names `notch_width` the manipulation-strength knob —
`Λ_w(x) = 1[x ≥ 0]` at `w = 0` is a true discontinuity, and `w = 0.25` with `ρ_cap = ∞` is the
smooth counterfactual with no discontinuity and no kink anywhere. The arms share `β` and `s`: they
are **equal-parameter, not equal-expected-value**, and the report must say so wherever it compares
them.

**Estimator backend.** Resolved through `gosplan.metrics.resolve_estimators()`, which imports
`forensics_core` inside a `try` / `except ImportError` and falls back to
`gosplan/metrics/_fallback.py` with identical signatures (PLAN §7.3). The resolved backend and
version go into the manifest (CONTRACT rule 10), so a bias curve is always traceable to the code
that produced it — and a curve computed on the fallback is labelled as such.

**Out of scope by PLAN §7.3, and therefore absent from this report:** digit tests; any claim of
*calibration* of archival detectors; any use of Phase-1 output for the archival anchor.

What PLAN does **not** decide, and what the revision must state before this card is issued:

- the settings grid's extent: PLAN names the three axes (excluded window, polynomial degree,
  bandwidth) but not their values;
- what "bandwidth" refers to for a binned polynomial counterfactual (bin width, or a smoothing
  bandwidth on top of it);
- the configuration the ten arms sit on top of — the post-G3 C0, or the Phase-1 configuration from
  `runs/G1_decision.md`;
- how the reconciliation power curve's x-axis (known fictitious-output share) is generated: by
  simulation at different `a · pen`, or by injection into a ledger.

## Forbidden

- Digit tests of any kind, and any claim that an estimator is *calibrated* on archival data (PLAN
  §§7.2, 7.3).
- Using Phase-1 output for the archival anchor (PLAN §7.3, "Out").
- Changing the pre-registered PLAN §4.5 settings. They are the centre of the grid and are recorded
  in the manifest; the grid explores around them, it does not replace them.
- Re-fitting the counterfactual polynomial until `b̂` looks right, or dropping arms whose CI
  coverage is poor. Poor coverage **is** the finding.
- Editing `gosplan/metrics/phenomena.py` or `_fallback.py` to make a curve come out; if a signature
  is wrong, that is an AMBIGUITY REPORT (rule 3).
- Importing `forensics_core` outside the `try` / `except ImportError` of PLAN §7.3.
- Computing any held-out phenomenon (PLAN §4.1 rows 2, 5, 6, 7) outside WO-030/WO-031.
- Running a gate from an implementer session (rule 13).
