Acceptance criteria PLAN §7.4 already fixes exactly:

- **design**: 2 framings (neutral vocabulary / historical vocabulary) × 3 payoff arms × ≥ 2 models ×
  20 episodes per cell. The three payoff arms, verbatim:

| Arm | Setting | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **baseline** | — | reference |
| **padding-dominated** | `audit_rate` `a = 1`, `penalty_scale` `pen` large | forwarding padded numbers is **strictly worse** for the ministry |
| **overfulfilment-optimal** | `ratchet_lambda` `λ = 0`, `overfulfilment_slope` `s` large | smoothing away overfulfilment is **strictly worse** |

- **the discriminating measure is whether ministry behaviour *tracks the payoff arm* (reasoning) or
  stays at the historical pattern regardless (retrieval)**. The framing contrast is **secondary**. A
  report that leads with the framing contrast has answered the wrong question;
- **manipulation check**: after each episode, in a **fresh context**, the model is asked to name the
  closest real-world analogue of the game it played; **results are stratified by whether it named
  Soviet planning**;
- **pinned model versions**; **every prompt and completion logged**; **strict-JSON responses with
  one retry and a documented fallback action (`π = 1`)**.

## Implementation notes

Provisional until the lead revises the spec and supplies the prompts.

The two non-baseline arms are constructed so that the *historically familiar* ministry behaviour is
strictly worse under them. That is what makes the arm contrast a test of reasoning rather than of
vocabulary: a model that pads under the padding-dominated arm is retrieving a pattern, not
optimising. The arm settings therefore may not be softened to keep the game "reasonable".

**Fallback accounting.** An episode in which the fallback (`π = 1`) fired is not a normal episode.
The rate of fallbacks per cell is a reported number, not a footnote: a model that fails to produce
parseable JSON under one framing and not another has told you something about the framing.

**Separation from the factorial.** PLAN §7.4's title says it. The LLM results are not an arm of
PLAN §4.3 and no `Δ_X` is computed from them.

**Manifest (CONTRACT rule 10).** `llm_models` carries the pinned ids and versions; a run without
them is not a result. The transcripts are artefacts of the run and are kept alongside it.

What PLAN does **not** decide, and what the revision must state before this card is issued:

- the prompts themselves (LEAD, PLAN §12.5);
- "pen large" and "s large" — the arms are specified qualitatively, and the actual values must be
  chosen so the strict-dominance claim is *verifiable*, ideally by the single-enterprise DP or by
  direct calculation, and then recorded;
- which ≥ 2 models, and their pinned versions (PLAN §15 item 8, WO-000);
- the operationalisation of "tracks the payoff arm": the statistic, its null, and the test;
- how the manipulation-check answers are coded into the "named Soviet planning" stratum, and by whom
  — a human coder, a rubric, or a second model — plus the inter-coder check on that coding.

## Forbidden

- Writing or editing any prompt. PLAN §12.5 assigns both framings and the manipulation check to the
  LEAD; a prompt written here would silently become the experiment.
- Running the manipulation check in the episode's own context, or letting its answer reach a
  ministry decision. PLAN §7.4 requires a **fresh context**.
- Softening a payoff arm; retrying an episode beyond the single permitted retry; dropping a fallback
  episode; omitting a logged prompt or completion.
- Reporting the framing contrast as the headline, or folding the LLM results into the factorial of
  PLAN §4.3.
- Unpinned model versions, or a table that does not carry them.
- Any claim about a real model's real-world behaviour beyond what this design measures: the
  manipulation check exists precisely because "the model recognised the setting" is a rival
  explanation, and it is reported as a stratum, not explained away.
- Computing any held-out phenomenon (PLAN §4.1 rows 2, 5, 6, 7) outside WO-030/WO-031.
- Running a gate from an implementer session (rule 13).
