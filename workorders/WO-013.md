Implementation notes

  1. INCORPORATE THE G0 FINDINGS. Read `runs/mc_sanity/report.md` and the rule-7 diff review, and
     apply to `spec/spec.py` only what those findings require. Each amendment is a line in the
     CHANGELOG entry naming the finding that produced it. A change with no G0 finding behind it is
     not a freeze, it is a redesign, and belongs to a separate lead decision.

  2. BUMP THE VERSION. `SPEC_VERSION = "1.0.0"` in `spec/spec.py`, and the identical mirror in
     `gosplan/config.py`. The two must agree: `EnvConfig.spec_version` defaults to the mirror and is
     written into every manifest (rule 10).

  3. WRITE THE CHANGELOG ENTRY in exactly the format `spec/CHANGELOG.md` prescribes, with all six
     fields - Reason (PLAN section plus the G0 finding or ambiguity report behind each change),
     Change (symbols added, removed or re-signed, before -> after), Affected work orders (every card
     whose whitelist, "Write only" list or must-pass tests are touched, INCLUDING COMPLETED CARDS
     THAT MUST BE REISSUED), Golden files (regenerated or not, and why), Suite (result of the full
     frozen suite after the change), Approver (LEAD, plus Human where the change crosses a gate or
     alters a pre-registered quantity of PLAN section 4). Move the "Unreleased" content into it and
     leave "Unreleased" empty.

     Two amendments are already owed to this entry, both recorded in the v0 file itself:
       - THE `Purpose` / `Dist` NARROWING. PLAN section 10 types `purpose` and `dist` as `str`;
         `spec/spec.py` narrows them to the `Purpose` and `Dist` literals, which enumerate exactly
         the values section 2.15 and the WO-004 card name. The v0 docstring says: "Record the
         narrowing in `spec/CHANGELOG.md` at the v1 freeze."
       - THE `selfobs` PURPOSE, added to `Purpose` for the observation noise of WO-008 (PLAN section
         2.4), beyond the list section 2.15 tabulates.

  4. GOLDEN FILES. Regenerate ONLY IF THE DYNAMICS CHANGED. Detect it, do not assume it:
     `uv run python -m ref.gen_golden --check` regenerates in memory and compares against the files
     on disk, writing nothing and exiting non-zero on any difference. If the check is clean, record
     "not regenerated" in the CHANGELOG. If the dynamics genuinely moved, regenerate with the
     unmodified generator (`uv run python -m ref.gen_golden`, no arguments - the pre-registered
     matrix of 5 configs x 3 seeds x 2 agents = 30 files, 30 agent-steps each) and say so, with the
     reason, in the entry. A golden set that is not the PLAN section 11 matrix is not a golden set:
     never `--force`, never a restricted `--config` / `--seed` / `--agent` filter.

  5. RE-RUN THE FULL SUITE and paste the result into the CHANGELOG's Suite field and into the
     session report.

  6. WHAT THIS CARD DOES NOT DO. It does not choose the Phase-1 values of the daggered PLAN section
     3 rows (`ratchet_lambda`, `growth_directive`, `overfulfilment_slope`, `penalty_scale`,
     `effort_cost`, `audit_rate`). Those are picked by the HUMAN at gate G1 from the interior of the
     DP regime map (WO-015) and recorded in `runs/G1_decision.md` - AFTER this card. The provisional
     placeholders stay exactly as they are. How, or whether, the G1 values are later folded into
     `p1_default_config()` and `gosplan/params.py` is a lead decision taken at G1 with its own
     CHANGELOG entry (rules 1 and 11); if that question blocks you here, it is an AMBIGUITY REPORT,
     not a guess.

  7. VERSION SEMANTICS AFTER THE FREEZE, restated from `spec/CHANGELOG.md`: patch = docstring,
     comment or type-alias clarification with no signature change; minor = additive and
     behaviour-preserving; major = any change to an existing signature, field, enum member or
     documented semantics. A major bump invalidates every golden file generated under the previous
     version.

Forbidden

  - Editing any test SOURCE file, `ref/ref_step.py`, `ref/gen_golden.py`, or any module under
    `gosplan/` other than the `SPEC_VERSION` mirror in `config.py`.
  - Hand-editing a golden `.json`, or regenerating the set to make a failing parity test pass. A
    parity failure that is not an intended dynamics change is a bug in `gosplan/env/` and BLOCKS THE
    FREEZE.
  - Bumping `SPEC_VERSION` without a complete CHANGELOG entry, or writing an entry missing any of
    the six required fields. Such an entry is not a valid change, and the change is reverted rather
    than documented after the fact.
  - Changing a parameter's arm in `gosplan/params.py` (rule 11) - not on this card at all.
  - Anticipating the G1 daggered values, or relabelling a provisional parameter as final.
  - Freezing while the suite is red, or while G0 has not been signed off in writing.
  - Touching `PLAN.md`. Running `tests/acceptance/` (rule 13).
