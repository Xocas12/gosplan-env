## Implementation notes

Provisional until the lead revises the spec.

Formulas, verbatim where PLAN decides them:

```
cost      c_ik = κ · e_ik² + F · 1[e_ik > 0] + κ_q · q_ik · e_ik            (§2.6)
bundle    X_bj += deliv_bj · q̄_j                                          (§2.7.3)
consumer  consumer_j = Σ_{i∈j} φ_j · shipped_i · q̄_i                      (§2.7.3)
measured  q̂_i = 1 + μ · (q̄_i − 1),  μ = information.quality_measurability  (§2.9.2)
          m_i = R_i · q̂_i   under objective_metric = quality_weighted
logged    val_true_t = Σ_i p_{s(i)} · y_i · q̄_i                           (§2.9.3)
```

What PLAN does **not** decide, and what the revision must state before this card is issued — each
is an AMBIGUITY REPORT if it is still open at issue time:

- how `q̄_i` is accumulated from the per-step `q_ik` over the `M` PRODUCE steps (output-weighted
  mean, plain mean, or last value) and how it is reset at the period boundary;
- whether the good-level `q̄_j` credited into a buyer's bundle is a shipment-weighted average over
  the selling sector or something else;
- how quality enters the coverage aggregator, if at all: `X` credited as `deliv · q̄_j` is a
  quantity adjustment, and whether `θ` sees adjusted or raw stocks changes the economics;
- the Phase-2 value of `quality_cost` (PLAN §3 gives 0 in Phase 1 and no P2 value).

Edge cases: `μ = 0` makes `q̂ ≡ 1` regardless of `q̄` — the Phase-1 identity, and the arm the row-3
pipeline check compares against; `quality_matters = False` must short-circuit before any of the
above runs.
