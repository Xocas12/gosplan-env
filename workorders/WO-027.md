## Implementation notes

Provisional until the LEAD writes the formulation.

What PLAN §6.2 already fixes and the formulation may not change:

- a **non-anticipative expected-value MIP** over the **full true state**. The planner-side
  information filters of PLAN §2.7.5 do not apply to the oracle: it is the benchmark of what the
  technology could deliver, not of what the planning system could learn;
- **mean yields** — the multiplicative shock of PLAN §2.6 is replaced by its mean of 1, which is
  exactly what makes the solution non-anticipative;
- **planning horizon = 12 periods** (`ORACLE_HORIZON_PERIODS`);
- **nonconvexities as configured**: `supply.setup_cost > 0` becomes a binary per enterprise-step;
  `supply.irs_alpha > 0` becomes a piecewise-linear approximation of
  `A_j(Kap) = A_j · (Kap / Kap_0)^{α_irs}`, whose breakpoints are recorded with the result because
  they are part of the formulation;
- the objective is welfare: the CES index of PLAN §2.9.3 on the consumer sink's receipts, averaged
  over the measured periods of PLAN §4.4;
- **solved once per configuration**, with HiGHS or CBC through OR-Tools or Pyomo;
- the **clairvoyant** branch solves the same programme per seed with the realised yield draws
  substituted for their means, and is reported **as an upper bound only**: `W_oracle` in PLAN §2.9.4
  is the expected-value MIP's welfare, and the clairvoyant number is never the denominator of
  `welfare_ratio` or `specification_gap`. A table showing both says which is which on the same line;
- the optimality gap is recorded in the manifest and never cleared by re-running with a looser
  tolerance.

What PLAN does **not** decide, and what the LEAD formulation must state before this card is issued:

- whether the CES objective is linearised (and how) or handed to the solver's nonlinear support —
  PLAN §6.2 explicitly defers this to the revision;
- the time limit and gap tolerance per solve, and which `status` values are acceptable;
- the treatment of the report/audit machinery in the oracle: an oracle that never misreports still
  has to be told what `val_oracle` means, since `specification_gap` divides by it;
- which solver is chosen, from `SOLVER_CANDIDATES`, and its pinned version.

## Forbidden

- Using the clairvoyant number as `W_oracle`, or reporting either without its label.
- Suppressing, rounding away or re-running away a non-zero optimality gap.
- Letting any oracle quantity reach an agent: `welfare_true` and the oracle solution are logged and
  never observed (CONTRACT rule 6).
- Importing a solver at module scope; the skeleton must import with the `solver` extra absent.
- Computing PLAN §4.1 rows **2, 5, 6, 7** — held out until WO-030.
- Substituting a heuristic upper bound for the MIP and calling it the oracle.
