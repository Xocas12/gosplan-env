## Implementation notes

Provisional until the lead revises the spec.

**Locked Phase-2 mechanism parameters (PLAN §4.2, row "2 Storming").** Locked *now*; not this
card's to change, nor WO-030's, nor WO-031's:

```
delivery_timing = "stochastic"
arrival_probs   = π_arrival = (0.25, 0.25, 0.25, 0.25)   over steps 0..3
yield_sigma     × 1 (the Phase-1 tuple, unchanged)
```

PLAN §4.2 states why the arrival distribution is uniform-random: there is then **no mechanical
backloading**, so any effort-Gini excess measured later is behavioural. Making the arrival
distribution late-weighted in the acceptance configuration would manufacture the very phenomenon
the study holds out; `backloaded` exists as a separate configuration, never as the acceptance value.

**Draw.** A delivered unit's arrival step is `k ~ Categorical(π_arrival)` through
`gosplan.rng.draw(seed_env, "arrival", …)` — purpose `arrival` is already in the `Purpose` enum of
`spec/spec.py` (PLAN §2.15). Keying must keep the draw order-independent, so the JAX port of WO-029
reproduces it and common random numbers hold across arms.

What PLAN does **not** decide, and what the revision must state before this card is issued:

- the index set of the draw — one arrival step per (buyer, good) delivery, per unit, or per
  (buyer, good, seller-pool); this changes the variance of coverage within a period;
- `EnvConfig.validate` requires `len(arrival_probs) == steps_per_period` when
  `delivery_timing != "uniform"`; at `steps_per_period = 8` (PLAN §3 grid) the locked 4-tuple no
  longer applies and the revision must say what does;
- the `backloaded` distribution itself ("π concentrated on late steps" is not a number);
- whether goods arriving at step `k` are usable at step `k` or only at `k + 1`.
