Implementation notes - PLAN section 5, verbatim

  SETTING. One enterprise; NO input-output (`a = 0`, `phi = 1`), so coverage is 1 and every claimed
  unit can be delivered; fixed capacity; geometric continuation `psi = cfg.incentive.tenure`.

  STATE GRID. `T` in `[T_min, grid.target_hi_mult * A * cap]`, 80 points, LOG-SPACED, with
  `T_min = cfg.tech.target_floor_frac * T_0` and `T_0 = cfg.tech.initial_target_frac * A * cap`.
  `S` in `[0, S_max]`, 50 points, LINEAR, with `S_max = cfg.tech.inventory_cap_mult * cap`.

  ACTION GRID. Period effort `e` in {0, 0.05, ..., 1} (21 points) - ONE EFFORT PER PERIOD, not per
  step: nothing within the period is observed, so uniform effort across the `M` steps is optimal and
  its cost is `M * kappa * e**2`. Report `rho` in [0, 3], step 0.02; the grid is EXTENDED
  AUTOMATICALLY when the optimum sits at the edge, and every edge hit is LOGGED AS A REGIME SIGNAL
  (`report_action_grid`, `report_grid_edge_fraction`, `DPSolution.rho_edge_frac`), never smoothed
  away.

  PERIOD YIELD. `y = A * cap * e * eps_bar` with `eps_bar ~ LogNormal(-sigma_bar**2 / 2, sigma_bar)`
  and `sigma_bar = sigma / sqrt(M)` - the aggregation approximation, WHICH MUST BE DOCUMENTED IN THE
  CODE AS AN APPROXIMATION. Take the expectation with 9-node GAUSS-HERMITE IN LOG SPACE
  (`grid.gh_nodes`); `numpy.polynomial.hermite.hermgauss` supplies nodes and weights, so no `scipy`
  at module scope.

  TRANSITION.
      S'  = (1 - h) * S + y
      R   = rho * T
      audit against S' with probability a and log-noise sigma_aud (S_hat = S' * exp(nu),
          nu ~ N(0, sigma_aud**2))
      S'' = S' - min(S', R)                      # delivery next period, phi = 1
      T'  = max(T_min, (1 + g) * T * (1 + lambda * clip(rho - 1, -c_dn, c_up)))

  BELLMAN.
      V(T, S) = max_{e, rho} { E_eps[ B(rho) - M * kappa * e**2 - a * E_nu[Pen] ]
                               + psi * gamma * E V(T', S'') }

  with `gamma = DP_DISCOUNT = 0.99`, the TECHNICAL discount, identical to `PPOConfig.gamma` because
  G2 criterion 1 compares the two policies directly and a discount mismatch would compare two
  different problems. `psi` is the ECONOMIC continuation probability `cfg.incentive.tenure`. They
  multiply here and are conflated nowhere else. Value iteration to `grid.value_tol` = 1e-6, or
  policy iteration - either is acceptable provided it reports the same `converged` flag and
  `n_iterations`.

  USE THE ENVIRONMENT'S OWN FUNCTIONS. `B(rho)` is `gosplan.env.reward.bonus(rho, cfg)` and the
  scale is `gosplan.env.reward.reward_scale(cfg)` - CALL THEM. Do not re-derive the notch, the
  overfulfilment slope or the cap: `Lambda_w` with `w = 0` is a strict `>=` Heaviside and
  `rho_cap = inf` means no clip, and those branches are already right there. This is also what keeps
  the regime map (WO-015) and the environment from ever drifting apart.

  THE PENALTY is the one place the environment's implementation cannot be called directly:
  `audit_and_penalise` takes a `State`, which the DP does not have. Evaluate the section 2.8 penalty
  on the DP's scalar state, covering BOTH `penalty_form` branches (`pen * f` and `pen * 1[f > 0]`)
  and BOTH `penalty_arg` branches (`f = max(0, R - S_hat) / T` and `f = |R - S_hat| / T`), with a
  comment citing section 2.8. DO NOT REFACTOR `gosplan/env/reporting.py` - it is not on your "Write
  only" list. If the two definitions could disagree, that is an AMBIGUITY REPORT, never a silent
  divergence.

  OUTPUTS - every field of `DPSolution`. `policy_effort` and `policy_rho` on the
  `(n_target, n_stock)` grid; `value`; the stationary distribution of `rho_report` from simulating
  the policy 200 EPISODES x 200 PERIODS (`simulate_stationary_reports`, burn-in per section 4.4);
  `b_hat_dp` (`dp_excess_mass`, computed with the PRE-REGISTERED SECTION 4.5 SETTINGS: bins of width
  0.005 on `rho` in [0.6, 1.4], excluded window [0.95, 1.02], polynomial degree 7, excess mass on
  [1.00, 1.02]); `fictitious_padding` = `mean max(0, R - S) / T`; `hidden_reserves` =
  `mean max(0, S - R) / T` after delivery; `mean_effort`; `regime`; `rho_edge_frac`; `n_iterations`;
  `converged`; `grid`; `config_hash = cfg.hash()`.

  `classify_regime` - THRESHOLDS VERBATIM (PLAN section 5):
      bunching                  mass of rho in [1.00, 1.005] > 0.5 AND mass at the grid edge < 0.05
      pad_to_cap                mass at the grid edge > 0.5
      truthful_underfulfilment  mean rho < 0.9 AND fictitious padding < 0.01
      mixed                     otherwise

  PERFORMANCE. Vectorise over the action grid (the `(e, rho)` outer product against the state grid).
  PLAN section 14 budgets about one CPU-minute per configuration at this resolution, and WO-015
  solves 500 of them.

  NON-CONVERGENCE IS A RESULT. A solution with `converged = False` is returned WITH THE FLAG and is
  never silently used as ground truth at gate G2. The same holds for `rho_edge_frac`: an edge hit is
  a regime signal, logged, never re-gridded away to make a label come out prettier.
