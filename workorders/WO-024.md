## Implementation notes

Provisional until the lead revises the spec.

**Locked Phase-2 values (PLAN §4.2, row "6 Blat")**, locked *now*: `horizontal_visibility = 1.0`,
`trade_tau` `τ = 0.05`.

**Offer semantics (PLAN §2.13).** `trade_offer_ij > 0` offers up to that fraction of `X_ij`;
`< 0` wants up to that fraction of `need_ij`. The two sides are scaled by different quantities
(stock for offers, need for wants) on purpose.

**Visibility.** A random subset of the other `N − 1` of size `horizontal_visibility · (N − 1)`,
drawn with purpose `trade_visibility` through `gosplan.rng.draw` (CONTRACT rule 9), redrawn per
period — visibility is an information parameter, not a fixed network.

**Matching and price.** Greedy by largest complementary pairs, executed at plan-price parity
(`p_s · Q_s = p_b · Q_b` from `state.plan_prices`), with a per-unit transaction cost `τ` on the
executed quantity, which physically disappears.

**Surplus, verbatim:**

```
Δh_i = p_{s(i)} · [ Ŷ_i(X_after) − Ŷ_i(X_before) ]
```

where `Ŷ_i` is next step's intended output at the agent's last effort, **computed by the
environment, never self-reported**. Wash trades are unprofitable because `τ > 0` and `Δh` is the
same function of the same true state for both sides.

What PLAN does **not** decide, and what the revision must state before this card is issued:

- the tie-breaking rule of the greedy matcher, and the iteration order that makes it deterministic
  and reproducible in `ref/ref_step.py`;
- whether the visibility mask is symmetrised, and how (PLAN says a trade needs both sides to see
  each other; the rule itself is not written);
- whether `τ` is charged in units of the good moved, on both legs or one, and to whom;
- the exact step at which `Ŷ` is evaluated — "next step's intended output at the agent's last
  effort" fixes effort but not the coverage state used for `need`;
- what happens at the last PRODUCE step of a period, where "next step" is the REPORT step.

## Forbidden

- Computing, plotting or testing PLAN §4.1 rows **2, 5, 6, 7** — held out. Row 6 (blat) is this
  mechanism's own phenomenon and is computed for the first time in WO-030, inside the P2 acceptance
  run of WO-031 only.
- Any reward term for trade other than the single `trade_surplus` of CONTRACT rule 4: no bonus for
  volume, for counterparty count, for reciprocity, and no self-reported gain.
- Letting `match_trades` write `inv_output`, `target`, any `last_*` field, `cum_output` or
  `cum_cost`. Trade reallocates inputs; it does not manufacture output, forgive a target or settle a
  claim.
- Any rule that makes enterprises trade, or that prices a trade so that trading is mechanically
  profitable. Blat must be a consequence of binding coverage plus visibility (rule 7).
- Direct `numpy.random` / `jax.random` under `gosplan/env/` (rule 9); a fixed counterparty network
  in place of the per-period keyed draw.
- Changing the PLAN §4.2 locked values.
