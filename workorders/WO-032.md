## Implementation notes

Provisional until the lead revises the spec.

**Arm construction.** Express each arm as a set of overrides applied to C0, so C0 is the only place
a configuration is written out and an arm can never drift from it silently. `C_AUDIT`'s
`audit_rate ×4` is a multiplier (`Multiplier` in the module), not an absolute value, and it must
respect the PLAN §3 range `[0.01, 0.30]`: at a post-G3 `audit_rate` above 0.075 the ×4 arm leaves
the registered sweep range, which is a reportable fact, not a value to clamp silently.

**Dual classification.** `audit_rate` is INFO *and* enters the reward through the penalty. PLAN §§3
and 4.3 both require it to be reported separately and never folded into C_OGAS; CONTRACT rule 11
makes the arm classification itself a design decision with a CHANGELOG entry.

**Convergence.** PLAN §6.3 — an arm whose exploitability exceeds the threshold is reported as
non-converged, not as a result. The contrast table carries that label per arm; a `Δ_X` computed from
a non-converged arm is printed with the label attached, or not printed at all.

**Price sensitivity** (PLAN §7.5) runs on this card's headline tables through WO-036: three
perturbed price vectors, fixed seeds, `specification_gap` sign changes reported first. The optional
Saltelli/Sobol design named in the same PLAN section is **WO-033**, a separate card.

What PLAN does **not** decide, and what the revision must state before this card is issued:

- the post-G3 C0 configuration itself;
- the bootstrap's stratification variable (seed, arm, or both) and the resample count;
- whether `Δ_X` CIs are computed on the paired difference under CRN (which is what CRN is for) or on
  the two marginals — PLAN fixes CRN and the CI method but not their composition.

## Forbidden

- Folding `audit_rate` or `audit_noise` into C_OGAS, or reporting C_OGAS without C_AUDIT beside it
  (PLAN §§3, 4.3).
- Reporting `Δ_OGAS` without `Δ_INC` and `I`.
- Changing an arm definition, adding an arm, or re-running an arm with different settings to move a
  CI. Parameter changes after G1 create a new, labelled study (PLAN §13).
- Reporting a non-converged arm as a result (PLAN §6.3).
- Dropping seeds, dropping episodes, or excluding reports at `ρ_max`; PLAN §4.4 includes them and
  flags them, and CONTRACT rule 8 forbids moving the bound.
- Computing any held-out phenomenon outside WO-030/WO-031, or re-deriving a metric this repository
  already computes.
- Running a gate from an implementer session (rule 13).
