## Implementation notes

Provisional until the lead revises the spec.

The report presents **two claims, stated separately** (PLAN §1.1). Merging them — reading a Claim-B
contrast as evidence for Claim A, or the reverse — is the single presentational failure this card
exists to avoid, and the section structure should make it impossible.

**Held-out phenomena (PLAN §§4.1, 4.2).** Each of rows 2, 5, 6, 7 appears with its pre-registered
operationalisation, the PLAN §4.2 locked parameters it was measured on, and its outcome. **A
phenomenon that failed to appear is reported as a failure, in the same table, in the same terms as
one that appeared.** It is never omitted, never relegated to an appendix, and never re-run at other
parameters to fill the cell.

**Failures generally (PLAN §13).** *"A gate that fails produces a written failure report."* Whatever
failure reports exist are part of the final report, not an annex to it.

**Sourcing (PLAN §15, WO-000).** Every parameter presented as historically motivated carries either
its sourced range or the sentence recorded in `docs/params_sources.md`; no point value is presented
as historical fact.

**Estimator provenance.** A bunching number computed on the vendored fallback rather than on
`forensics_core` is labelled with the backend and version from the manifest (PLAN §7.3).

What PLAN does **not** decide, and what the revision must state before this card is issued:

- the report's medium and structure, and whether figures are regenerated or collected;
- the enumeration of "every headline table" — shared with WO-036; one list, in one place;
- whether the optional Sobol table is included when the design was not run, and how its absence is
  stated.

## Forbidden

- Recomputing any metric. This card reads tables and manifests; a number that appears in the report
  and nowhere upstream is a defect.
- Omitting, softening or moving to an appendix a failed held-out phenomenon, a non-converged arm, a
  `BOUND_BINDING` flag, a non-zero oracle gap, or a `specification_gap` sign change.
- Presenting the clairvoyant bound as `W_oracle`.
- Presenting a Claim-B contrast as evidence for Claim A, or merging the two claims.
- Presenting any unsourced parameter as historically sourced (PLAN §15).
- Digit tests, or any claim of calibration of archival detectors (PLAN §§7.2, 7.3).
- Running a gate from an implementer session (rule 13). This card writes the generator; the lead
  runs it and the human signs off.
