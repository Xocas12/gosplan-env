THE CRITERIA - PLAN section 4.5, verbatim

  2. BUNCHING PRESENT / ABSENT (N = 20). At the NOTCHED configuration, `b_hat >= 0.5 * b_hat_DP`
     (the DP-predicted excess mass FOR THE SAME PARAMETERS) with bootstrap CI EXCLUDING 0, in
     >= 90% OF 30 SEEDS. At the SMOOTH counterfactual, the CI for `b_hat` COVERS 0 in >= 90% of
     seeds.
  3. PADDING ELASTICITY. Across the three `a*pen` values, learned fictitious padding is MONOTONE
     DECREASING and WITHIN 0.03 of the DP's at each.
  4. HYGIENE. `BOUND_BINDING` flag ABSENT IN ALL RUNS; `T > 3*T_0` reached in < 5% OF TRAINING
     EPISODES AFTER 20% OF TRAINING.

  Encoded as `BHAT_DP_FRACTION = 0.5`, `SEED_PASS_FRACTION = 0.90`, `N_SEEDS = 30`,
  `PADDING_DP_TOL = 0.03`, `PADDING_MONOTONE_IN = "audit_rate * penalty_scale"`,
  `BOUND_FLAG = "BOUND_BINDING"`, `TARGET_RUNAWAY_MULT = 3.0`, `TARGET_RUNAWAY_MAX_FRAC = 0.05`,

Implementation notes

  1. ARMS (PLAN sections 2.8, 4.5). Two schedules, EQUAL-PARAMETER rather than equal-expected-value:
     both share `notch_height` and `overfulfilment_slope` with the G1-recorded configuration and
     differ only in the two fields of `ARMS` - `notched` = (`notch_width = 0.0`,
     `overfulfilment_cap = 1.2`), a true discontinuity plus a kink at the cap; `smooth` =
     (`notch_width = 0.25`, `overfulfilment_cap = inf`), no discontinuity and no kink anywhere
     (`inf` means no clip at all).

  2. PROCEDURE.
       a. Train `N_SEEDS = 30` runs per arm on the G1-recorded configuration at `N = 20` with the
          WO-018 harness, logging every step to a `Ledger` and writing `runs/<hash>/manifest.json`
          with the estimator version and the reference-PPO version (rule 10). ONE `seed_env` FOR
          BOTH ARMS, so they meet identical environment draws (CRN, sections 2.15 and 4.3) and the
          contrast is within-draw.
       b. CRITERION 2. Per seed, evaluate over the measurement window (`t >= 2`) and call
          `phenomenon_bunching` (WO-016) WITH ITS PRE-REGISTERED DEFAULTS. Notched condition:
          `b_hat >= BHAT_DP_FRACTION * b_hat_dp` with the bootstrap CI excluding 0. Smooth
          condition: the CI covers 0. Each must hold in at least `SEED_PASS_FRACTION` of seeds.
          Report HOLE MASS on [0.95, 1.00) alongside, as PLAN section 4.1 row 1 requires.
       c. CRITERION 3. Per level in `ap_levels`, evaluate `phenomenon_padding` and compare the
          learned fictitious padding with the DP's at the same level: monotone decreasing in
          `audit_rate * penalty_scale`, and within `PADDING_DP_TOL` at each. `audit_rate` is
          dual-classified - INFO, and also entering the reward through the penalty - which is why
          THE COMPOUND, not either factor, is the sweep variable and why the elasticity is always
          reported against it BY NAME.
       d. CRITERION 4. `BOUND_FLAG` absent from EVERY run's manifest, and the fraction of training
          episodes reaching `T > TARGET_RUNAWAY_MULT * T_0` - with
          `T_0 = tech.initial_target_frac * A_{s(i)} * cap_i` - AFTER the first
          `HYGIENE_WINDOW_START_FRAC` of training below `TARGET_RUNAWAY_MAX_FRAC`. The 20% window is
          pre-registered so the early transient of finding F4 is excluded by pre-registration rather
          than by inspection.
       e. PRICE SENSITIVITY. Run the standing check of PLAN sections 2.9.4 and 7.5 on this
          experiment's headline table through `gosplan.experiments.price_sensitivity.run` and
          include its table in the report. A sign change in `specification_gap` is REPORTED, NEVER
          SUPPRESSED. NOTE: that module is the Phase-3 card WO-036 and may still be a skeleton at
          issue time. If it is, the report records the check as "not run - WO-036 pending" and you
          FILE AN AMBIGUITY REPORT. DO NOT RE-IMPLEMENT PRICE SENSITIVITY INSIDE `phase1_gate.py`.
       f. Write `table.parquet` (per arm and seed: `b_hat`, CI bounds, hole mass, flags) and
          `report.md` with one pass/fail line per criterion, the seed counts behind each, the
          estimator settings and versions actually used, and every flag raised.

  3. THE SECTION 4.5 ESTIMATOR SETTINGS HAVE EXACTLY ONE HOME - `gosplan/metrics/phenomena.py`
     (WO-016) - and are recorded in the manifest from there. THIS MODULE MUST NOT RESTATE THEM.

  4. MEASUREMENT. Periods `t >= 2` of every episode; no end-of-episode exclusion under geometric
     termination; reports at `rho_max` INCLUDED in the histograms and FLAGGED (rule 8).

  5. HELD OUT. Rows 2, 5, 6 and 7 of PLAN section 4.1 are not computed, plotted or tested here.
     Phase 1 touches rows 1 and 4 only; WO-030 computes the held-out rows for the first time, in the
     Phase-2 acceptance run.

  6. COST (PLAN section 14). 2 arms x 30 seeds at `N = 20`, about 5M environment steps at roughly 3k
     steps/s in NumPy (about 30 minutes per run) - about 30 CPU-hours, about 4 hours on 8 cores.

  7. EXIT CODE. `main()` never means "G2 passed": G2 is signed off by Human and LEAD across both
     reports.
