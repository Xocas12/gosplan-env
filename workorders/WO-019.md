THE CRITERION - PLAN section 4.5, criterion 1, verbatim

  1. DP RECOVERY (single enterprise, N = 1, a = 0). For three values of `a*pen` (chosen at G1 to
     span the bunching region), PPO's mean fictitious padding is within 0.02 (RATIO UNITS) of the
     DP's, mean effort within 0.05, and the Wasserstein-1 distance between PPO's and the DP's
     stationary `rho_report` distributions is below 0.03 - IN >= 8 OF 10 SEEDS PER VALUE.

     FAILURE OF (1) IS A TRAINING-STACK FAILURE AND BLOCKS EVERYTHING.

  Encoded as `PADDING_TOL = 0.02`, `EFFORT_TOL = 0.05`, `WASSERSTEIN_TOL = 0.03`,
  `SEEDS_PER_LEVEL = 10`, `MIN_PASSING_SEEDS = 8`, `N_AP_LEVELS = 3`. The three levels' VALUES are
  read from `runs/G1_decision.md`; they are deliberately not constants in the module, because a
  value chosen after training would invert the pre-registration.

  PASS/FAIL LINES the report must carry, one per level, in this form:

      level a*pen = <value>:  PASS  - 9/10 seeds within all three tolerances
                                      (padding |d| max 0.014 <= 0.02; effort |d| max 0.031 <= 0.05;
                                       W1 max 0.022 < 0.03)
      level a*pen = <value>:  FAIL  - 7/10 seeds (needs >= 8); W1 exceeded on seeds 3, 5, 9
      CRITERION 1: FAIL - training-stack failure; everything downstream is blocked.

  A level with 7 passing seeds FAILS. The criterion is never restated as a mean over seeds, never as
  a median, and never as "8 of 10 on two of three levels".

Implementation notes

  1. SETTING (PLAN section 5, verbatim). One enterprise, no input-output, fixed capacity, geometric
     continuation `psi = cfg.incentive.tenure`. The comparison configuration is the G1-recorded
     Phase-1 configuration REWRITTEN to `supply.n_enterprises = 1`, an all-zero `io_matrix` and
     `final_demand_share` all ones (`a = 0`, `phi = 1`) - a rewriting, not a fixed literal, because
     `J` comes from that configuration. THE SAME `EnvConfig` OBJECT goes to
     `solve_single_enterprise` and to the training harness, so the DP and the environment cannot
     drift; the DP already calls the environment's own `bonus`.

  2. PROCEDURE.
       a. Per level: build the recovery configuration and solve
          `solve_single_enterprise(cfg_level, DPGrid())` ONCE. Read off `fictitious_padding`,
          `mean_effort`, `stationary_rho`.
       b. Train `SEEDS_PER_LEVEL` PPO runs on the same configuration with the WO-018 harness at the
          WO-017 adapter's fixed hyper-parameters, logging every step to a `Ledger` and writing
          `runs/<hash>/manifest.json` (rule 10, reference-PPO version included).
       c. Evaluate each trained policy over the measurement window (`t >= 2`, section 4.4; at-bound
          reports included and flagged) and compute per seed: mean fictitious padding, mean effort,
          and the Wasserstein-1 distance between the evaluated `rho_report` samples and the DP's
          `stationary_rho`.
       d. A SEED passes when `|padding_ppo - padding_dp| <= 0.02` AND
          `|effort_ppo - effort_dp| <= 0.05` AND `wasserstein1 < 0.03`. A LEVEL passes when at least
          8 of 10 seeds pass. CRITERION 1 passes when EVERY level passes.
       e. Write `table.parquet` (one row per level and seed) and `report.md` with the comparison
          table, the distances, and the pass/fail line per level above.

  3. CRN. Seeds are `seed_env` offset per seed index and shared between the DP simulation and the
     PPO evaluation, so the comparison runs under common random numbers (sections 2.15, 4.3).

  4. WASSERSTEIN-1 via `scipy.stats.wasserstein_distance`, or an equivalent exact one-dimensional
     computation on the sorted samples - imported INSIDE the function that computes it, never at
     module scope.

  5. COMPARED QUANTITIES ARE EXACTLY THREE (`COMPARED_QUANTITIES`): `fictitious_padding`,
     `mean_effort`, `stationary_rho_wasserstein1`. Adding a fourth after seeing a result would
     change the criterion after the fact.

  6. COST (PLAN section 14). 3 levels x 10 seeds at `N = 1`, about 2M environment steps - about 1
     GPU-hour or about 8 CPU-hours.

  7. A `DPSolution` WITH `converged = False` is never used as ground truth: report the flag and
     stop.

  8. EXIT CODE. `main()` never means "G2 passed": G2 is signed off by Human and LEAD across BOTH
     this report and `runs/phase1_gate/report.md` (WO-020).
