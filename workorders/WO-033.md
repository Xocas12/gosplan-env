## Implementation notes

Provisional until the lead revises the spec.

**Factor list.** 13 swept INFO+INC parameters, read from `gosplan/params.py` (arm and range), never
hand-transcribed — the registry is the single source, and CONTRACT rule 11 makes its arm
classification a design decision. *Which* 13, exactly, is the revision's to state: PLAN §3 lists
more than 13 INFO and INC rows, several of which are fixed rather than swept (`ratchet_cap_up` /
`ratchet_cap_dn` "fixed", `alloc_eta_need` "fixed"), and `audit_rate` is dual-classified.

**Sampling.** Saltelli's extension of the Sobol sequence over the unit hypercube, mapped through
each factor's PLAN §3 range. Categorical factors (`objective_metric`, `aggregation_level`,
`audit_mode`, `penalty_form`, `penalty_arg`) have no natural continuous range and the revision must
say whether they are in the design at all.

**Outcomes.** The three headline metrics of PLAN §2.9.4, over the measurement window of PLAN §4.4.

**Manifest (CONTRACT rule 10).** One manifest per run; the design's own record carries `N_base`, the
factor list, the ranges used and the index estimator, so an index can be traced to its design.

What PLAN does **not** decide, and what the revision must state before this card is issued:

- the exact 13 factors, and the treatment of categorical ones;
- whether second-order indices are computed (the run count differs by ≈1,300);
- the index estimator and its convergence check;
- how a run that fails (non-finite metric, non-converged policy) is handled — dropping it silently
  biases the index, so the rule must be written down.

## Forbidden

- Reporting an index without the sweep ranges in the same table (PLAN §4.3, bold).
- Widening a factor's range to make an index look larger, or narrowing it to make one look smaller.
  The ranges are the registry's; changing one is a CHANGELOG matter (rule 11) and a new, labelled
  study (PLAN §13).
- Substituting a cheaper sensitivity method and calling it Sobol, or reporting first-order indices
  where PLAN asks for total-order.
- Running the design on the NumPy path because JAX is unavailable. PLAN §14: "JAX only". If the port
  is not ready, the correct outcome is that this optional card does not run.
- Computing any held-out phenomenon outside WO-030/WO-031.
- Running a gate from an implementer session (rule 13).
