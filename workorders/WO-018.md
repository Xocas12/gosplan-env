Implementation notes

  What the card requires, and therefore what this module contains: a VECTORISED ENVIRONMENT BATCH,
  CHECKPOINTS, PERIODIC EVALUATION WITH THE LEDGER, THE MANIFEST, CRN BY `seed_env`, WALL-CLOCK
  LOGGING, and the ENTROPY ANNEAL 0.01 -> 0.001.

  1. `TrainConfig` HAS NO SIZING DEFAULTS, ON PURPOSE. PLAN fixes the TECH hyper-parameters
     (`gamma`, `lambda_GAE`, the learning rate, the clip coefficient, the entropy endpoints - all in
     `PPOConfig`) but not the batch shape or the training budget; those follow the experiment card
     that commissions the run (WO-019's `N = 1` comparison, WO-020's `N = 20` gate) inside the PLAN
     section 14 envelope. Inventing a default here would make an unrecorded experimental choice look
     like a specification (rule 3).

  2. BATCH AND SEEDS. `make_env_batch(cfg, n_envs, seed_env)` builds `n_envs` copies of `GosplanEnv`
     stepped in lockstep; the batch axis is outer, the `N` enterprises inner. Seeds derive from
     `seed_env`, so arms share environment draws by construction (sections 2.15, 4.3), and
     `seed_policy` stays a separate stream (rule 9). Evaluation uses its OWN seed block, so it never
     consumes the training sequence.

  3. UPDATES AND THE ANNEAL. `n_updates = total_agent_steps // (n_envs * rollout_steps)`;
     `entropy_coefficient(update, n_updates, ppo_cfg)` interpolates `entropy_coef_start` ->
     `entropy_coef_end` (0.01 -> 0.001) over exactly that count. An agent-step is one PRODUCE or
     REPORT step, so a period is `cfg.incentive.steps_per_period + 1` of them; a rollout that is not
     a whole number of periods is legal - the update boundary may fall inside a period - and the
     manifest records it.

  4. PERIODIC EVALUATION. `evaluate(...)` runs `eval_episodes` episodes with the ledger attached and
     computes ROWS 1 (bunching) AND 4 (padding) ONLY (WO-016), over the section 4.4 window
     (`t >= 2`, no end-of-episode exclusion, at-bound reports included and flagged). Checkpoint
     selection may
     NOT be driven by `welfare_true` or `val_measured` (rule 6).

  5. MANIFEST (rule 10). `train_manifest_extra` supplies `reference_ppo_version` (from
     `IPPO.manifest_entry`), `estimator_backend` and `estimator_version` (from
     `resolve_estimators`), `bunching_settings` (from `phenomena.py`), the seeds, the flags and the
     `TrainConfig` itself. Every field of `MANIFEST_FIELDS` is present; inapplicable ones are
     `null`.

  6. NO REWARD TOUCHING. The environment returns the rule-4 reward, already multiplied by the
     analytic `reward_scale(cfg)`. Store it, compute GAE from it, hand it to the reference PPO. Do
     not normalise, clip, shape or rescale it, and DO NOT WRAP THE ENVIRONMENT IN ANYTHING THAT
     RESCALES IT.

  7. NO HELD-OUT PHENOMENON. Rows 2, 5, 6 and 7 are held out: no plot, table or test of them before
     the Phase-2 acceptance run (PLAN section 4.1), not in a learning curve, not in a debug print.

  8. NO SILENT BOUND CHANGE. If the ledger raises `BOUND_BINDING` (more than 1% of reports at
     `rho_max`), the flag rides in the manifest and every report of the run displays it. The bound
     is never widened to clear it (rule 8).

  9. CHECKPOINTS AND LOGGING. `runs/<env_cfg.hash()>/checkpoints/` via `checkpoint_path`, every
     `checkpoint_every_updates`. `log_update` appends one row per update with the wall clock, so the
     PLAN section 14 throughput estimate (about 3k steps/s in NumPy) can be checked against reality.

Forbidden

  - Any reward normalisation, clipping, shaping or rescaling, in the harness or in any wrapper it
    applies (rule 4).
  - Feeding `StepInfo`, `State`, `PlannerView`, `welfare_true` or `val_measured` to the learner, or
    selecting a checkpoint by any of them (rule 6).
  - Computing, plotting or logging any PLAN section 4.1 row other than 1 and 4.
  - Inventing a default for any sizing field of `TrainConfig`, or changing a `PPOConfig` TECH value.
  - Widening `report_max_ratio` or suppressing `BOUND_BINDING` (rule 8).
  - Implementing or forking PPO here - the learner is the pinned reference the adapter wraps.
  - Editing `gosplan/agents/ppo/adapter.py`, `gosplan/env/`, `gosplan/metrics/`, `tests/`, `ref/`,
    `spec/spec.py` or `PLAN.md`. Running `tests/acceptance/` (rule 13).
